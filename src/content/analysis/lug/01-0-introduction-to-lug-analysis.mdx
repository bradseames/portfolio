### 9.1 Introduction to Lug Analysis

Lugs are connector-type elements widely used as structural supports for pin connections. In the
past, the lug strength was overdesigned since weight and size requirements were for the most
part unrestricted. However, the refinements of these requirements have necessitated conservative
methods of design.

This section presents static strength analysis procedures for uniformly loaded lugs and bushings,
for double shear joints, and for single shear joints, subjected to axial, transverse, or oblique
loading. Also listed is a section which applies to lugs made from materials having ultimate
elongations of at least 5% in any direction in the plane of the lug. Modifications for lugs with
less than 5% elongation are also presented. In addition, a short section on the stresses due to
press fit bushings is presented.
