### 9.4.2 Pin Shear Strength for Double Shear Joints Under Uniform Axial Load

The pin ultimate shear load $ (\text{P}_{{\text{us}}_{{\text{p}}}}) $ for the symmetrical
joint shown in Figure 9-5 is the double
shear strength of the pin:

$$
\text{P}_{{\text{us}}_{{\text{p}}}} = 1.571 \ {{\text{D}}_\text{p}}^2 \ \text{F}_{{\text{su}}_{
{\text{p}}}} \qquad  \text{(9-12)}
$$

where $  \text{F}_{{\text{su}}_{{\text{p}}}} $ is the ultimate shear stress of the pin material.
