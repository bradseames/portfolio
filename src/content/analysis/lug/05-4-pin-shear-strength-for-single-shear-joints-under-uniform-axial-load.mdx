### 9.5.4 Pin Shear Strength for Single Shear Joints Under Uniform Axial Load

The maximum value of pin shear can occur either within the lug or at the common shear face of
the two lugs, depending upon the value of $ \text{M}/\text{Pt} $ At the lug ultimate load the
maximum pin shear stress $ (\text{F}_{{\text{s}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}}) $ is approximated by

$$
\text{F}_{{\text{s}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}} = \frac{{\text{1.273 P}}}{{\text{D}_\text{p}}^2}
\ , \ (\text{if } \frac{\text{M}}{\text{P t}} \leq 2/3) \qquad \text{(9-26a)}
$$

$$
\text{F}_{{\text{s}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}} = \frac{{\text{1.273 P}}}{{\text{D}_\text{p}}^2} +
\frac{\sqrt{(\frac{2 \text{M}}{\text{P t}})^2 + 1} - 1}{\frac{2 \text{M}}{\text{P t}} + 1 - \sqrt{(\frac{2 \text{M}}{\text{P t}})^2 + 1}}
\quad , (\text{if } \frac{\text{M}}{\text{P t}} \gt 2/3) \qquad \text{(9-26b)}
$$

Equation (9-26a) defines the case where the maximum pin shear is obtained at the common shear
face of the lugs, and Equation (9-26b) defines the case where the maximum pin shear occurs away
from the shear face.

The allowable ultimate value of $ \text{F}_{{\text{s}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}}  $ is
$ \text{F}_{{\text{su}}_{{\text{p}}}} $ , the ultimate shear stress of the pin material.
