### 9.5.2 Lug.Net-Section Strength for Single Shear Joints Under Uniform Axial Load

At ultimate load the nominal value of the outer fiber tensile stress in the lug net-section is
approximated by

$$
\text{F}_{{\text{t}}_{{\text{max}_\text{L}}}} =
\frac{\text{P}}{(\text{w-D})\text{t}} + \frac{6 \ {\text{M}}}{{\text{K}_{\text{b}_{\text{L}}} \
(\text{w-D})\text{t}^2}}
\qquad \text{(9-24)}
$$

where
$\text{K}_{\text{b}_{\text{L}}}$ is the plastic bending coefficient for the lug net-section.

The allowable ultimate value of
$\text{F}_{{\text{t}}_{{\text{max}}}}$ is
$ \text{F}_{{\text{nu}}_{{\text{L}}}} $ (Equation (9-4)) or
$ 1.304 \ \text{F}_{{\text{ny}}_{{\text{L}}}} $ ,(Equation (9-5)), whichever is lower.
