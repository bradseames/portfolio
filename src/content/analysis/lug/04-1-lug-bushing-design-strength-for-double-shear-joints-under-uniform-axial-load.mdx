### 9.4.1 Lug-Bushing Design Strength for Double Shear Joints Under Uniform Axial Load

The allowable lug-bushing ultimate load
$ (\text{P}_{{\text{u}}_{{\text{L}_\text{B}}}} ) $ for the joint is computed, using Equation
(9-10). For the symmetrical joint shown in the figure, Equation (9-10) is used to calculate the
ultimate load for the outer lugs and bushings
$ (2\text{P}_{{\text{u}}_{{\text{L}_\text{B1}}}} ) $ and the ultimate load, for the inner lug
and bushing
$ (\text{P}_{{\text{u}}_{{\text{L}_\text{B2}}}} ) $. The allowable value of
$ (\text{P}_{{\text{u}}_{{\text{L}_\text{B}}}} ) $ for the joint is the lower of these two values.
