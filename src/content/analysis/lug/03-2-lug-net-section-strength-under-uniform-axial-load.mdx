### 9.3.2 Lug Net-Section Strength Under Uniform Axial Load

The allowable lug net-section tensile ultimate stress $ (\text{F}_{nu_L}) $ on Section 1-1 in
Figure 9-4a is affected by the ability of the lug material to yield and thereby relieve the
stress concentration at the edge of the hole.

$$
\text{F}_{nu_L} = \text{K}_\text{n} \ \text{F}_\text{tu}
$$

$ \text{K}_\text{n} $, the net-tension stress coefficient, is obtained from the graphs shown in
Figure 9-4 as a function of the ultimate and yield stress and strains of the lug material in the
direction of the applied load. The ultimate strain $ (\varepsilon_{u}) $ can be obtained from
MIL-HDBK-5.
