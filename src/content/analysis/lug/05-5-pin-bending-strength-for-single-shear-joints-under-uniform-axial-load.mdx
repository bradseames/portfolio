### 9.5.5 Pin Bending Strength for Single Shear Joints Under Uniform Axial Load

The maximum pin bending moment can occur within the lug or at the common shear faces of the two
lugs, depending on the value of M/Pt. At the lug ultimate load the maximum pin bending stress
$ (\text{F}_{{\text{b}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}}) $ is approximated by

$$
\text{F}_{{\text{b}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}} = \frac{{\text{10.19 M}}}{{{\text{k}}_{{_\text{br}}}
\  \text{D}_\text{p}}^3} \left[ \frac{{\text{Pt}}}{2 \text{M}} - 1 \right]
\ , \ (\text{if } \frac{\text{M}}{\text{P t}} \leq 3/8) \qquad \text{(9-27a)}
$$

$$
\text{F}_{{\text{b}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}} = \frac{{\text{10.19 M}}}{{{\text{k}}_{{_\text{br}}}
\  \text{D}_\text{p}}^3} +
\frac{\sqrt{(\frac{2 \text{M}}{\text{P t}})^2 + 1} - 1}{\frac{2 \text{M}}{\text{P t}}}
\quad , (\text{if } \frac{\text{M}}{\text{P t}} \gt 3/8) \qquad \text{(9-27b)}
$$


where $ \text{k}_{\text{br}} $ is the plastic bending coefficient for the pin.

Equation (9-27a) defines the case where the maximum pin bending moment is obtained at the common
shear face of the lugs, and Equation (9-27b) defines the case where the maximum pin bending
moment occurs away from the shear face, where the pin shear is zero.

The allowable ultimate value of $ \text{F}_{{\text{b}}_{{\text{max}_\text{p}}}} $ is
$ \text{F}_{{\text{tu}}_{{\text{p}}}} $, the ultimate tensile stress of the pin material.
