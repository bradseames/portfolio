## 9.3 Lug and Bushing Strength Under Uniform Axial Load

Axially loaded lugs in tension must be checked for bearing strength and for
net-section strength. The bearing strength of a lug loaded in tension, as
shown in Figure 9-1, depends largely on the interaction between bearing,
shear-out, and hoop-tension stresses in the part of the lug ahead of the pin.
The net-section of the lug through the pin must be checked against
net-tension failure. In addition, the lug and bushing must be checked to
ensure that the deformations at design yield load are not excessive.
