## 9.7 Lug and Bushing Strength Under Transverse Load

Transversely loaded lugs and bushings are checked in the same general manner as axially loaded
lugs. The transversely loaded lug, however, is a more redundant structure than an axially loaded
lug, and it has a more complicated failure mode. Figure 9-7 illustrates the different lug
dimensions that are critical in determining the lug strength.
