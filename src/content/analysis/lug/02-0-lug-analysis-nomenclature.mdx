### 9.2 Lug Analysis Nomenclature

$\text{F}_{\text{bru}_{\text{L}}}$ = Lug ultimate bearing stress

$\text{F}_{\text{bry}_{\text{L}}}$ = Lug yield bearing stress

$\text{F}_{\text{tux}}$ = Cross-grain tensile ultimate stress of lug material

$\text{F}_{\text{tyx}}$ = Cross-grain tensile yield stress of lug material

$\text{F}_{\text{bru}}$ = Allowable ultimate bearing stress, MHB5

$\text{F}_{\text{bry}}$ = Allowable yield bearing stress, MHB5

$\text{F}_{\text{tu}}$ = Ultimate tensile stress

$\text{F}_{\text{nu}_{\text{L}}}$ = Allowable lug net-section tensile ultimate stress

$\text{F}_{\text{ny}_{\text{L}}}$ = Allowable lug net-section tensile yield stress

$\text{F}_{\text{bry}_{\text{B}}}$ = Allowable bearing yield stress for bushings

$\text{F}_{\text{cy}_{\text{B}}}$ = Bushing compressive yield stress

$\text{F}_{\text{bru}_{\text{B}}}$ = Allowable bearing ultimate stress for bushings

$\text{F}_{\text{su}_{\text{P}}}$ = Ultimate shear stress of the pin material

$\text{F}_{\text{tu}_{\text{P}}}$ = Pin ultimate tensile stress

$\text{F}_{\text{tu}_{\text{T}}}$ = Allowable ultimate tang stress

$\text{F}_{\text{br}_{\text{max}_\text{L}}}$ = Maximum lug bearing stress

$\text{F}_{\text{br}_{\text{max}_\text{B}}}$ = Maximum bushing bearing stress

$\text{F}_{\text{s}_{\text{max}_\text{P}}}$ = Maximum pin shear stress

$\text{F}_{\text{b}_{\text{max}_\text{P}}}$ = Maximum pin bending stress



$\text{P}_{\text{bru}_{\text{L}}}$ = Allowable lug ultimate bearing load

$\text{P}_{\text{nu}_{\text{L}}}$ = Allowable lug net-section ultimate load

$\text{P}_{\text{u}_{\text{B}}}$ = Allowable bushing ultimate load

$\text{P}_{\text{u}_{\text{L}}}$ = Allowable design ultimate load

$\text{P}_{\text{u}_{\text{L}_\text{B}}}$= Allowable lug-bushing ultimate load

$\text{P}_{\text{us}_{\text{P}}}$ = Pin ultimate shear load

$\text{P}_{\text{ub}_{\text{P}}}$ = Pin ultimate bending load

$\text{P}_{\text{ub}_{\text{P}_\text{max}}}$ = "Balanced design" pin ultimate bending load.

$\text{P}_{\text{all}}$ = Allowable joint ultimate load

$\text{P}_{\text{T}}$ = Lug tang strength

$\text{P}_{\text{tru}_{\text{L}}}$ = Allowable lug transverse ultimate load

$\text{P}_{\text{tru}_{\text{B}}}$ = Allowable bushing transverse ultimate load

$\text{K}_{\text{n}}$ = Net-tension stress coefficient

$\text{K}_{\text{b}_{\text{P}}}$ = Plastic bending coefficient for pin

$\text{K}_{\text{b}_{\text{T}}}$ = Plastic bending coefficient for tang

$\text{K}_{\text{br}_{\text{L}}}$ = Plastic bearing coefficient for lug

$\text{K}_{\text{b}_{\text{L}}}$ = Plastic bending coefficient for lug

$\text{K}_{\text{tru}}$ = Transverse ultimate load coefficient

$\text{K}_{\text{try}}$ = Transverse yield load coefficient


$\text{M}_{\text{max}_{\text{P}}}$ = Maximum pin bending moment

$\text{M}_{\text{u}_{\text{P}}}$ = Ultimate pin failing moment

$\text{A}$ = Area, $\text{in}^2$

$\text{a}$ = Distance from edge of hole to edge of lug, inches

$\text{B}$ = Ductility factor for lugs with less than 5% elongation

$\text{b}$ = Effective bearing width, inches

$\text{D}$ = Hole diameter of pin diameter, inches

$\text{E}$ = Modulus of elasticity, psi

$\text{e}$ = Edge distance, inches

$\text{f}$ = Stress, psi

$\text{f}_{\text{a}}$ = Cyclic stress amplitude on net section of given lug, $\text{lbs/in}^2$

$\text{f}_{\text{m}}$ = Mean cyclic stress on net section of given lug, $\text{lbs/in}^2$

$\text{f}_{\text{max}}$= Maximum cyclic stress on net section of given lug, $\text{lbs/in}^2$

$\text{f}_{\text{min}}$ = Minimum cyclic stress on net section of given lug, $\text{lbs/in}^2$

$\text{g}$ = Gap between lugs, inches

$\text{h}_1 \text{...} \text{h}_2$ = Edge distances in transversely loaded lug, inches

$\text{h}_{\text{ave}}$ = Effective edge distance in transversely loaded lug

$\text{K}$ = Allowable stress (or load) coefficient

$\text{k}_1, \text{k}_2, \text{k}_3$ = Fatigue parameters

$\text{M}$ = Bending moment, in-lbs

$\text{N}$ = Fatigue life, number of cycles

$\text{P}$ = Load, lbs

$\text{R}$ = Stress ratio, $\text{f}_{\text{min}} / \text{f}_{\text{max}}$

$\text{t}_{\text{B}}$ = Bushing wall thickness, inches

$\text{t}$ = Lug thickness, inches

$\text{w}$ = Lug width, inches

$\alpha$ = Angle of load to axial direction, degrees

$\varepsilon$ = Strain, inches/inch

$\rho$ = Density, $\text{lbs/in}^3$
